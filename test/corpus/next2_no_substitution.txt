===============================================================================
C2-I-062: an arrow inside braced tildes is text in a strike
===============================================================================
a {~old~>new~} b

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  (paragraph
    (strikethrough
      (strikethrough_begin)
      (content)
      (strikethrough_end))))
