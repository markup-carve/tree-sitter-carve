C2-I-087: a footnote label holding a bracket ends at its first bracket
================================================================================

[^[x]]() and [x [^[]] y]()

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  (paragraph
    (footnote_reference
      (footnote_marker_begin)
      (reference_label)
      (footnote_marker_end))

      (footnote_marker_begin)
      (reference_label)
      (footnote_marker_end))))
