=========================
Nested extension marker stays literal before the first closing bracket
=========================
:x[a :x[b] c]

---

(document
  (paragraph
    (extension_inline
      (extension_marker_begin)
      (content))))

=========================
The extension cut implicitly closes code inside its payload
=========================
:x[a `b] c` d]

---

(document
  (paragraph
    (extension_inline
      (extension_marker_begin)
      (content
        (verbatim
          (verbatim_marker_begin)
          (content)
          (verbatim_marker_end))))
    (verbatim
      (verbatim_marker_begin)
      (content)
      (verbatim_marker_end))))

=========================
A table caption uses the same extension payload cut
=========================
| h |
^ :x[a
b :x[z]]

---

(document
  (table

      (table_cell))
